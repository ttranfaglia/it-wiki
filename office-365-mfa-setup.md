# Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for Office 365

*Last updated: 10/5/2026 · Applies to: Office 365 / Microsoft 365 sign-in*


Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds a second check when you sign in, such as a notification on your phone. Even if someone learns your password, they can't get in without it.

## What you need

- Your work or school account (for example, **you@napacoe.org**)
- A computer, and your smartphone (iPhone or Android)
- About 5 minutes

## Recommended: Microsoft Authenticator app

### Step 1: Install the app on your phone

1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
2. Search for **Microsoft Authenticator** and install it.
3. Open it and allow notifications when asked.

### Step 2: Start setup on your computer

1. Go to **[https://office.com](https://office.com)** and sign in with your work or school account.
2. Click on Manage My Account ->Security Info.
3. Otherwise select **Add sign-in method**, then **Authenticator app**.

### Step 3: Link your phone

1. On your computer, select **Next** until you see a **QR code**.
2. On your phone, open Microsoft Authenticator and tap **+** (Add account), then **Work or school account**, then **Scan a QR code**.
3. Scan the QR code on your computer screen.
4. On your computer, select **Next**.

### Step 4: Approve a test sign-in

1. A notification appears on your phone. Open it and enter the number shown on your computer screen.
2. Approve it (you may need your phone's PIN, fingerprint or face).
3. Your computer shows **Notification approved**. Select **Next**, then **Done**.

You're set. The next time you sign in to Office 365 from a new place or device, approve the prompt on your phone.

## Everyday use

- **Approve a sign-in:** Open the notification, type the number shown, and approve. Only approve prompts that you started.
- **If you didn't try to sign in:** Tap **Deny**, then change your password and contact IT right away.
- **Trusted devices:** Choosing **Don't ask again for [14] days** on your own device means fewer prompts.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| No notification on my phone | Open the Authenticator app and pull to refresh, check your internet connection, and make sure notifications are allowed. |
| The number doesn't match / no number appears | Wait a minute and try again. Cancel and sign in again to get a new prompt. |
| The code is rejected | Check your phone's date and time are set to automatic. |
| I got a new phone | [Submit a Zendesk Ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/) |
| I lost my phone | Contact TTSS right away so they can reset your MFA and secure your account. |
| I'm locked out of the security info page | [Submit a Zendesk Ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/) or contact TTSS. |

## Security reminders

- Never share a code or approve a prompt for someone else, even if they say they're IT.
- Approving a prompt you didn't start is how many accounts get taken over. If in doubt, deny it.
- Keep your phone locked with a PIN, fingerprint or face.
