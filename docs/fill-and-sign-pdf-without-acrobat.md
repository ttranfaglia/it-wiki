# Fill Out and Sign a PDF Without Adobe Acrobat

*Last updated: 10/6/2026 · Applies to: Windows and Mac · Google Chrome*

You can type into a PDF form, add your own text, and sign it using Google Chrome. No Adobe Acrobat and no paid software needed.

## What you need

- The PDF you need to complete
- Google Chrome, updated to the latest version
- A mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen to draw your signature
- About 5 minutes

## Before you start: which kind of signature?

| Kind | What it is | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Drawn signature** | An image of your handwriting placed on the page | Most internal forms, timesheets, acknowledgments |
| **Digital (certificate) signature** | A secure, verifiable signature tied to your identity | Only when the form specifically asks for one |

The steps below make a **drawn signature**. It is a visual mark, not a verified digital signature, and it has no audit trail. If the document asks for a digital or certificate-based signature, or says it must be signed with DocuSign or a similar service, follow the sender's instructions or [submit a Zendesk ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/).

## Fill out and sign in Google Chrome (Windows and Mac)

### 1. Open the PDF in Chrome

Right-click the PDF, choose **Open with**, then **Google Chrome**. (See **Open and Read a PDF Without Adobe Acrobat** if you need help.)

### 2. Fill in the form fields

1. Click a field and type. Press **Tab** to move to the next field.
2. For check boxes and radio buttons, click them.
3. For drop-down lists, click the field and choose an item.

### 3. Add text where there is no field

Some PDFs are just a picture of a form with no fields to click.

1. Select **Add text** in the toolbar.
2. Choose a font, style and color in the sidebar.
3. Click where you want the text and type.

### 4. Draw your signature

1. Select **Draw** in the toolbar (the scribble icon).
2. In the panel on the right, select **Pen**. Choose a **thin line** and a dark color (black or dark blue looks best).
3. Hold the left mouse button and sign in the signature area. On a laptop you can use the trackpad, and on a touchscreen use your finger or a pen. Zoom in first for better control.
4. If you make a mistake, select **Undo** or the **Eraser** and click the line, then draw again.
5. Select **Done** when you finish.

### 5. Save your completed copy

1. Select **Download** in the toolbar.
2. Choose **With your changes**. This is important: **Without your changes** saves the blank original.
3. Give the file a new name, such as **form-signed.pdf**, so you keep the original.
4. Open the saved file once to confirm your text and signature show up.

If your Google Drive is available, you can also select **Save to Drive**. Use your work account only, and don't save work forms to a personal Google account.

## Send the signed PDF

1. Attach the saved PDF to a new Outlook email.
2. Check the file name and the recipient before you send.
3. For sensitive forms, ask the recipient how they would like to receive it. Do not email passwords, Social Security numbers or other secrets unless the recipient confirms that is the approved method.

## Mac alternative: Preview

If you're on a Mac and would rather use a saved signature you can reuse, **Preview** can capture one with your trackpad or camera.

1. Open the PDF in **Preview**.
2. Select **Show Markup Toolbar** (the pen tip icon), then select **Sign**.
3. Choose **Trackpad** or **Camera**, create your signature, then select **Done**.
4. Click your signature to place it. Drag to move it, and drag a corner to resize.
5. Select **File**, then **Save**. Use **File**, then **Duplicate** first if you want to keep the original.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| I can't find Draw or Add text | Your Chrome may be out of date. Select the three dots, then **Help** > **About Google Chrome**, and restart when asked. If the tools are still missing, [submit a Zendesk ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/). |
| I can't type in the form | The PDF may have no fillable fields. Use **Add text** instead. |
| My typing or signature is missing from the saved file | You probably chose **Without your changes**. Download again and choose **With your changes**. |
| My typing doesn't show in another app | Some apps do not show form fields or markup. Flatten the file (see below). |
| The signature looks messy | Select **Undo** and redraw. A thin line, a slow steady hand, and zooming in help. |
| The text is too big or too small | Select **Undo** and add it again with a different size in the sidebar. |
| The recipient says it isn't signed | Make sure you downloaded **With your changes** and sent that saved file. |
| The form says it needs Adobe Reader | Some older forms use a special format that only Adobe can fill in. [Submit a Zendesk ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/) and we will find another way. |
| A "Sign" or "Certificate" button is gray | That form needs a digital signature, which is a different process. Contact the sender or IT. |

### Lock your changes in place (flatten)

Flattening turns your typing and signature into part of the page, so they can't be moved or edited.

1. Press **Ctrl + P** (**Cmd + P** on Mac).
2. Under **Destination**, choose **Save as PDF**.
3. Select **Save** and give the new file a name.

## Staying safe

- Only sign documents you have read and understand.
- Keep your signature to yourself. Don't save an image of it where others could copy it. Don't email a signature image.
- Don't upload work documents to free online PDF "fill and sign" websites, and don't install PDF extensions from the Chrome Web Store without asking us. The files may be stored by a company outside our control. The tools described here run inside Chrome on your computer.
- If a PDF or email asks you to sign in to Microsoft or Google or "verify" your account, do not. Report it to us.
