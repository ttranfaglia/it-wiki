# Fill Out and Sign a PDF Without Adobe Acrobat

*Last updated: 10/6/2026 · Applies to: Windows and Mac · Microsoft Edge, Mac Preview*

You can type into a PDF form, add your own text, and sign it using the tools already on your computer. No Adobe Acrobat and no paid software needed.

## What you need

- The PDF you need to complete
- Microsoft Edge (Windows or Mac) or Preview (Mac)
- A mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen to draw your signature
- About 5 minutes

## Before you start: which kind of signature?

| Kind | What it is | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Drawn signature** | An image of your handwriting placed on the page | Most internal forms, timesheets, acknowledgments |
| **Digital (certificate) signature** | A secure, verifiable signature tied to your identity | Only when the form specifically asks for one |

The steps below make a **drawn signature**. If the document asks for a digital or certificate-based signature, or says it must be signed with DocuSign or a similar service, follow the sender's instructions or [submit a Zendesk ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/).

## Fill out and sign in Microsoft Edge (Windows and Mac)

### 1. Open the PDF in Edge

Right-click the PDF, choose **Open with**, then **Microsoft Edge**. (See **Open and Read a PDF Without Adobe Acrobat** if you need help.)

### 2. Fill in the form fields

1. Click a field and type. Press **Tab** to move to the next field.
2. For check boxes and radio buttons, click them.
3. For drop-down lists, click the field and choose an item.

### 3. Add text where there is no field

Some PDFs are just a picture of a form with no fields to click.

1. Select **Add text** in the toolbar.
2. Click where you want the text and type.
3. Drag the text box to move it. Use the size options to make it bigger or smaller.

### 4. Draw your signature

1. Select **Draw** in the toolbar.
2. Choose a **color** (dark blue or black looks best) and a **thin line width**.
3. Hold the left mouse button and sign in the signature area. On a laptop you can use the trackpad, and on a touchscreen use your finger or a pen.
4. If you make a mistake, select **Erase** and click the line, then draw again.
5. Select **Draw** again to turn drawing off.

### 5. Save your completed copy

1. Select **Save** (the disk icon) or **Save as** in the toolbar.
2. Save the file with a new name, such as **form-signed.pdf**, so you keep the original.
3. Open the saved file once to confirm your text and signature show up.

## Fill out and sign on a Mac with Preview

### Fill in the form

1. Open the PDF in **Preview**.
2. Click a field and type your information.
3. If you can't type into the form, select **Show Markup Toolbar**, then use the **Text** tool (the box with **T** in it) and click where the text should go.

### Create and add your signature

1. Select **Show Markup Toolbar** (the pen tip icon), then select **Sign**.
2. Choose how to create it:
   - **Trackpad:** Click **Trackpad**, sign with your finger, press any key, then select **Done**.
   - **Camera:** Sign your name on white paper, hold it to the camera and select **Done**.
   - **iPhone or iPad:** Choose the device option and sign on its screen.
3. Click your signature to place it. Drag to move it. Drag a corner to resize.

### Save

Select **File**, then **Save**. To keep the original, use **File**, then **Duplicate** first and work on the copy.

## Send the signed PDF

1. Attach the saved PDF to a new Outlook email.
2. Check the file name and the recipient before you send.
3. For sensitive forms, ask the recipient how they would like to receive it. Do not email passwords, Social Security numbers or other secrets unless the recipient confirms that is the approved method.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| I can't type in the form | The PDF may have no fillable fields. Use **Add text** (Edge) or the **Text** tool (Preview). |
| My typing doesn't show in another app | Save the PDF, close it and reopen it. Some apps do not show form fields or markup. Print to PDF to flatten them (see below). |
| The signature looks messy | Select **Erase** (Edge) and redraw, or recreate the signature in Preview. A thin line and a slow, steady hand work best. |
| The text is too big or too small | Click the text box and use the size options. |
| The recipient says it isn't signed | Make sure you **saved** the file after drawing. Send the saved copy. |
| The form says it needs Adobe Reader | Some older forms use a special format that only Adobe can fill in. [Submit a Zendesk ticket](https://napacoe.zendesk.com/) and we will find another way. |
| A "Sign" or "Certificate" button is gray | That form needs a digital signature, which is a different process. Contact the sender or IT. |

### Lock your changes in place (flatten)

Flattening turns your typing and signature into part of the page, so they can't be moved or edited.

1. Press **Ctrl + P** (**Cmd + P** on Mac).
2. Choose **Microsoft Print to PDF** (Windows), or **Save as PDF** (Mac).
3. Select **Print** or **Save** and give the new file a name.

## Staying safe

- Only sign documents you have read and understand.
- Keep your signature to yourself. Don't save an image of it where others could copy it. Don't email a signature image.
- Don't upload work documents to free online PDF "fill and sign" websites. The files may be stored by a company outside our control.
- If a PDF or email asks you to sign in to Microsoft or "verify" your account, do not. Report it to us.
