# Reset or Change Your Network Password (AD / ADSelfService Plus)

*Last updated: 10/5/2026 · Applies to: school/work computer and Office 365 sign-in*


Your network (Active Directory) password is the one you use to sign in to your computer and, in most cases, to Office 365. You can change or reset it yourself using the self-service portal. You do not need to call IT unless you get stuck.

## Which one do I need?

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| I know my password and want a new one | [Change your password](#change-your-password) |
| I forgot my password | [Reset your password](#reset-your-password-forgot-it) |
| My account is locked | [Unlock your account](#unlock-your-account) |
| I never set up self-service | [Enroll first](#first-time-setup-enroll) |

## First-time setup (enroll)

Do this once, while you can still sign in. It lets you reset your own password later.

1. Go to **[https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do](https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do)**.
2. Sign in with your usual username and password.
3. Choose **Enroll** (or follow the prompt that appears).
4. Pick and answer your verification methods, such as security questions, a mobile number or an authenticator app.
5. Save. You should see a message that enrollment is complete.

> Tip: Choose answers only you know.

## Change your password

Use this when you know your current password.

1. Go to **[https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do](https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do)** and sign in.
2. Select **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Select **Submit**.
5. Sign out and sign back in. Update the password on your phone, tablet and any saved Wi-Fi or mail apps.

On a Windows computer you can also press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete** and choose **Change a password**.

## Reset your password (forgot it)

1. On the sign-in screen, or at **[https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do](https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do)**, select **Reset Password** (or **Forgot password?**).
2. Enter your username.
3. Verify your identity using the methods you enrolled (security questions, a code sent to your phone or email, or an authenticator app).
4. Enter a new password twice.
5. Select **Submit**, then sign in with the new password.

## Unlock your account

Too many wrong attempts will lock your account for a short time ([LOCKOUT LENGTH, e.g. 30 minutes]).

1. Go to **[https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do](https://adss.ncoe.k12.ca.us/authorization.do)** and choose **Unlock Account**.
2. Verify your identity.
3. Wait a moment, then sign in again.

Old saved passwords on a phone or laptop are the most common cause of repeated lockouts. After a password change, update every device.

## Password rules

- Can not contain the user's account name or parts of the user's full name that exceed two consecutive characters
- At least **14** characters
- Contain characters from three of the following four categories:
	English uppercase characters (A through Z)
	English lowercase characters (a through z)
	Base 10 digits (0 through 9)
	Non-alphabetic characters (for example, !, $, #, %)
- Cannot reuse your last 5 passwords
- Expires every 90 days / does not expire

A passphrase of several unrelated words is easier to remember and harder to guess.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| "Password doesn't meet requirements" | Check the rules above, and don't reuse an old password. |
| Reset works but Office 365 still rejects it | Wait up to [15] minutes for the change to sync, then sign out of Office 365 and sign in again. |
| I didn't receive the verification code | Check spam, check the phone number is correct, and try another method. |
| I can't verify my identity | Contact IT (see below). Have a photo ID or other proof ready. |
| I keep getting locked out | Remove old saved passwords on all your devices, then unlock again. |

## Keep your account safe

- Never share your password, even with IT/TTSS. IT/TTSS will never ask for it by email or chat.
- Don't reuse it on personal websites.
- If you think someone else knows it, change it now and tell IT.
